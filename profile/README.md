# PerlOps

### Automate. Secure. Modernize.

**Modern Perl. Enterprise Engineering. Agentic AI.**

PerlOps is an independent open-source initiative focused on building practical, modern engineering tools for enterprise automation, cybersecurity, system integration, infrastructure operations, and legacy modernization.

Our goal is to bridge the gap between proven technologies and modern engineering practices, combining the power of Perl with cloud-native architectures, intelligent automation, and artificial intelligence.

---

## Our Mission

- Build practical tools that solve real-world engineering challenges.
- Demonstrate the relevance of Perl in modern enterprise environments.
- Bridge legacy systems with modern technologies.
- Enable secure, reliable, and intelligent automation.
- Contribute to the open-source ecosystem through reusable software, documentation, and knowledge sharing.

## Engineering Domains

| Domain | Focus |
